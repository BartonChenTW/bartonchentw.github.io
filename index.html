Curriculum vitae

Yi-Chung Barton Chen

Energy systems researcher with a background in chemical engineering and materials science, and postdoctoral research experience in the UK and Switzerland. At Empa’s Urban Energy Systems Laboratory, I lead energy system modelling from individual buildings to the national scale. My current focus is preparing real-world energy data for modelling and AI through open and FAIR approaches.
